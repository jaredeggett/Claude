Read style: slow, deadpan, dry, mid-30s/40s male, slight rasp.
Reference: Donald Glover narration energy, not announcer energy.
Confidence in the absurdity. Pauses on punctuation. No upspeak.

Mic: Shure SM7B, Rode NT1, or any decent USB mic in a closet with hanging
clothes for sound dampening. Audacity or equivalent. Record each line 3–4
times, pick the strongest.
