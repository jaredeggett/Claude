# SORA-02 — Rotating Product Hero

Insert at: 1:22–1:26 (4 seconds, generated at 6 seconds for trim flexibility)
Purpose: Apple-style hero shot to anchor the Kickstarter tier reveal.

## Primary prompt

```
Cinematic product hero shot of a single matte cream-white circular wireless
button, approximately 60mm diameter, 16mm thick, with a subtle inset rocker
face and beveled edges. The button rotates slowly on its vertical axis on a
seamless cream-colored backdrop. Lit from one key light at 30 degrees and a
softer fill from the opposite side, with a gentle rim light catching the
top edge. The button completes one full slow rotation over 6 seconds. No
text, no logos visible, no shadows on the backdrop. Color palette: cream,
warm whites, subtle gold highlights from the key light. Photorealistic,
advertising quality, like an Apple product hero. 6 seconds.
```

## Variants

1. Same shot but with the button on a polished black surface (more dramatic).
2. Same shot but with the button slowly tumbling end-over-end instead of rotating.
3. Same shot but with three buttons in a triangular arrangement, all rotating together.

Generate 3–5 variants total. Pick the strongest.

## Edit notes

Music swells slightly during this insert. No VO over the rotation. Cut back
to whiteboard on the next VO line ("First hundred backers feed at $19.99.").

This shot also doubles as a looping background for the Kickstarter campaign
page during the live campaign.
