# SORA-01 — Macro Button Press

Insert at: 0:24–0:28 (4 seconds)
Purpose: Hardware credibility. The single most important Sora insert.

## Primary prompt

```
Cinematic macro shot, 240fps slow motion, of a single matte cream-white
circular wireless button being pressed by an adult finger. The button is
approximately 60mm diameter, sits on a warm wood desk surface, lit from a
soft window light at 45 degrees. The finger descends from upper frame,
presses the button with a subtle compression and bounce-back, releases. As
the press happens, a barely-visible bloom of warm light flares from the
button edge for a single frame. Background is shallow depth of field,
slightly blurred wood grain. No text, no logos, no other objects in frame.
Color palette: cream, soft warm whites, subtle wood tones. Photorealistic,
hyperreal, advertising quality. Single continuous shot, no cuts. 4 seconds.
```

## Variants

1. Same shot but on a clean white desk instead of wood.
2. Same shot but with a black coffee mug slightly out of focus in the corner.
3. Same shot but lit cooler (morning blue light instead of warm).

Generate 3–5 variants total. Pick the strongest.

## Edit notes

End the preceding whiteboard shot on a quick push-in zoom on the drawn
button, then cut to this Sora macro shot. Cut back from Sora with a matched
zoom-out so it reads as the camera "diving into" the drawing.
