# SORA-03 — Molty Moment

Insert at: 1:28–1:29 (1 second)
Purpose: Brand-character beat right before the final close.

## Primary prompt

```
2D animated character shot of "Molty" the lobster, hand-drawn whiteboard
illustration style. Molty is a friendly cartoon lobster with simple black
ink line work, two black dot eyes, two front claws. He sits centered in
frame on a plain white background. Over 1 second, Molty's left eye closes
in a slow deliberate wink, then opens again. The line work is loose,
hand-drawn, slightly imperfect, matching the aesthetic of RSA Animate or a
real whiteboard sketch. Marker-style black lines on pure white. No color
except the linework, no shading, no gradients. 1 second, smooth animation,
ends with both eyes open.
```

## Variants

1. Same shot but Molty raises one claw in a small wave instead of winking.
2. Same shot but Molty does a single antenna twitch.
3. Same shot but Molty's eyes flick to the right and back (knowing glance).

## Fallback

If Sora cannot match the whiteboard line style cleanly, draw Molty's wink
as a 6-frame loop in Procreate and skip Sora for this shot. The line-work
match is more important than the source of the asset.
